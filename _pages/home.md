## Research

My research lies at the intersection of human–computer interaction (HCI), mobile and ubiquitous computing, and sensing. I explore how to seamlessly integrate sensing of human behavior and physiology into everyday life, with an emphasis on using off-the-shelf sensors and devices and developing robust AI models. My interests include:

- **Wearable interfaces:** Creating intuitive and delightful interactions between people and wearable devices.
- **Everyday healthcare:** Developing technologies that reduce the cost and effort of healthcare, making care accessible at home and in daily life.
- **Deployment and empirical studies:** Deploying technologies in real-world settings and getting insights by evaluating their usability, effectiveness, and users' experiences.
